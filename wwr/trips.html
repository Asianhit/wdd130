Featured Excursions

Salmon River Gorge

Duration: 1 Day | Difficulty: Class III

Perfect for beginners and families alike! Enjoy a day filled with scenic canyon views, splashy intermediate rapids, and a delicious riverside lunch provided by our expert guides.

Snake River Canyon

Duration: 3 Days | Difficulty: Class IV

An action-packed multi-day journey through towering rock walls. Challenge yourself against roaring rapids by day, and relax around a roaring campfire under the stars by night.

The Big Drops Extreme

Duration: 5 Days | Difficulty: Class V

Designed strictly for experienced thrill-seekers. Face some of the most powerful and technical rapids in the country. This intense expedition will test your limits and leave you breathless.
